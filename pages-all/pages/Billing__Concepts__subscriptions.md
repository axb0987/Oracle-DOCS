# Subscriptions

The Subscriptions page provides subscriptions detail, usage information, billing schedule, and rate card information.

## Viewing Subscriptions

To view subscriptions, open the navigation menu and click Billing &amp; Cost Management . Under Billing , click Subscriptions .

The Subscriptions page lists all current and past[Infrastructure](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/infrastructure_subscriptions.htm)and[Applications](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/applications_subscriptions.htm)subscriptions associated with your cloud account.
Note  
  
See[Deleting Your Account](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/../Tasks/changingpaymentmethod.htm#Terminating_Your_Account)for more information on billing impact and deleting a cloud account.

Click a subscription to view the Subscription information[details page](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/applications_subscriptions.htm).
- For[Infrastructure](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/infrastructure_subscriptions.htm)subscriptions, you can review your usage, billing schedule, and rate card information.
- For[Applications](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/applications_subscriptions.htm)subscriptions, you can see service details and usage metrics.
Note  
  

- Credit quantity and Fulfilled value are 0 for a Pay As You Go subscription, because there's no committed amount, and you're paying for what you use. For customers in monthly/annual commit plans, however, the billing schedule line entries add up to the value in these fields.
- Service details and usage metrics aren't available for[Multicloud Universal Credits](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/multicloud/universal-credits.htm)subscriptions.

On the Subscriptions page, the following information fields are displayed:

Column Description
Name ID and name of the subscription. Click the link to open the Subscription information page.
Type

The subscription type, which can be Infrastructure or Applications:
- Infrastructure : Oracle Cloud Infrastructure Universal Credit Model subscriptions. View usage, billing schedule, and rate card information.
- Applications : Pertains to all SaaS Applications services. View service details and usage metrics.
Status Status of the subscription.
- Awaiting fulfillment
- Awaiting customer activation
- Fulfillment complete
- Active
- Terminated
- Canceled
- Suspended
Start Date (UTC) The subscription start date according to the UTC time zone.
Renewal Date (UTC) The subscription end date according to the UTC time zone.
