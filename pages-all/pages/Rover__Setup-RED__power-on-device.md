# Power On the Device

After you've configured your controlling device, power on the device.
- 

Power up the device by pressing the power button. See[Identify Front and Rear Panel Items](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Rover/Setup-RED/rear_panel_identification.htm#rear_panel_identification).

On Roving Edge Devices, the LED by the power button blinks for a few seconds and then turns solid green.

The serial console is displayed on your terminal device.
