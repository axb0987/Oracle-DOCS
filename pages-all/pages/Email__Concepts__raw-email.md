# Sending Raw Email

Create and send highly customized raw emails without restrictions, making it a powerful tool for all the email marketing and transactional needs.

For a streamlined development experience, we recommend using the HTTPS interface, especially with the[SubmitRawEmail](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/api/#/en/emaildeliverysubmission/latest/EmailRawSubmittedResponse/SubmitRawEmail)API, as it provides a restful endpoint and various authentication options, making it easier to integrate email sending capabilities into your applications.
