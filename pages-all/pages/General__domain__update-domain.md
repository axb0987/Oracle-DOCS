You can only update the email address for an active domain:
- On the Domain Management page, find the domain that you want to work with. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/General/domain/list-domain.htm).
- From the Actions menu for the domain, select Update Email . An Update Email window is displayed.
- In the Domain Notification Email field, enter the new email address and select Save .
