# Can't Add a Schedule to a Snapshot Policy

When adding a schedule to a snapshot policy, a "duplicate" error occurs.

Each schedule in a snapshot policy must be a combination of type, effective from, and snapshot retention duration that is unique to the policy. For more information, see[Schedule Types and Timing](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/File/Troubleshooting/../Tasks/snapshot-policies-and-schedules.htm#snapshot-policies-and-schedules__schedule-types).
