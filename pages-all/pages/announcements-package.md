## Announcements

Manages OCI console announcements. Announcements are those that OCI displays in the Console. Console announcements appear at the top of the page to communicate timely, meaningful information about service status. An announcement's prefix helps you understand, at a glance, the type and relative severity of the information and whether there's anything you can or must do.
