# Deactivating a Sign-On Policy

Deactivate a sign-on policy.

- On the Sign-on policies list page, select aq sign-on policy that you want to deactivate. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Sign-On Policies](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/signonpolicies/list-signon-policy.htm).
- From Actions menu, select Deactivate sign-on policy .
