# Setting up Email Submissions

Use OCI Email Delivery for a fast and reliable managed solution to submit secure, high-volume marketing, and transactional emails using the Hypertext Transfer Protocol Secure (HTTPS) interface or the Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) interface.

OCI Email Delivery is optimized for bulk marketing or transactional emails and ensures the best sending reputation.
