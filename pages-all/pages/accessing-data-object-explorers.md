# Accessing Data Object Explorers

Each data object explorer is accessed from within the context of its Ops Insights functional area as follows.

SQL Explorer

SQL Explorer only shows you SQL-related data objects.
- Open the navigation menu, click Observability &amp; Management , and then click Ops Insights .
- In the left pane, click Database Insights , SQL Insights , and then click SQL Explorer .

Data Object Explorer

Data Object Explorer shows you all available data objects.
- Open the navigation menu, click Observability &amp; Management , and then click Ops Insights .
- In the left pane, click Data Object Explorer .

Exadata Explorer

Exadata Explorer only shows you Exadata-related data objects.
- Open the navigation menu, click Observability &amp; Management , and then click Ops Insights .
- In the left pane, click Exadata Insights , and then click Exadata Explorer .

AWR Hub Explorer
- Open the navigation menu, click Observability &amp; Management , and then click Ops Insights .
- In the left pane, click Database Insights , and then click AWR Hub .
- Click Actions and then select Launch AWR Hub Explorer .
