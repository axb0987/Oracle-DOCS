# Clearing Your Keep Me Signed-In Sessions

If you choose to use Keep me signed in , then you can view the details for your Keep me signed-in sessions and clear any Keep me signed-in sessions that you no longer want to keep.

Keep me signed in must be enabled by your administrator for your identity domain.

- Go to your My Profile console page, and select Keep me signed-in sessions . If you need help finding the My Profile console page, see[Getting Your Profile Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/usersettings/get-details-my-profile.htm).
- Select the Saved passwords tab and find Keep me signed in sessions on the page.
- Select one or more signed-in session to clear, and then select Clear session .
