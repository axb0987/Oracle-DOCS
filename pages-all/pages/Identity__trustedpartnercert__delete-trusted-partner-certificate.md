# Deleting a Trusted Partner Certificate

Delete a trusted partner certificate from an identity domain in IAM.

- On the Trusted partner certificates list page, select the certificate that you want to remove. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Trusted Partner Certificates](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/trustedpartnercert/list-trusted-partner-certificate.htm).
- Select Delete .
