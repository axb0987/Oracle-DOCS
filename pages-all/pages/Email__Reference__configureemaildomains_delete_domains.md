# Deleting an Email Domain

Delete an email domain.

- [Console](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/configureemaildomains_delete_domains.htm#)
- [CLI](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/configureemaildomains_delete_domains.htm#)
- [API](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/configureemaildomains_delete_domains.htm#)
- 

- On the Email Domains list page, click the email domain that you want to delete. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Email Domains](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/list-email-domains.htm).
- From Actions menu for the email domain, select Delete .
- When prompted, confirm the deletion.
The email domain is deleted.
-
