# Marketplace

Use this section to learn how Marketplace content must be evaluated for Dedicated Region workloads and what architects must validate before adoption.

Marketplace in OCI Dedicated Region provides a controlled way to deploy approved Oracle-created software listings. The experience operates similarly to OCI Marketplace in the public cloud, but approved publishers and listings are limited for business, legal, and regulatory reasons.

Dedicated Region supports Oracle-created Marketplace listings, including free, Bring Your Own License (BYOL), and paid listings that use Universal Credit Model (UCM) consumption. These listings can include Oracle software that is not console-native, such as Oracle WebLogic Server.

Third-party Marketplace listings and SKUs are not supported in Dedicated Region. Self-published Windows and Red Hat software, self-published customer or partner software, custom rate cards, self-published support mechanisms, and white-labeled partner or ISV experiences are also not supported.

Oracle-created listings are enabled as a complete catalog. Individual Oracle listings cannot be enabled or disabled separately. Approved listings and SKUs can change over time.

Paid Oracle listings are subject to the applicable commercial terms. Existing customers might require a contract addendum before using paid Marketplace listings. A standard Marketplace consumption limit of 5% can apply to paid listings and is set during contract signing. The limit is based on total contract value for the realm, not on a single year or an individual region.

Free and BYOL listings do not have a Marketplace UCM spending limit. UCM allocated for paid Marketplace listings can also be used for other standard consumable Dedicated Cloud SKUs, subject to the customer's contract. Discounts for underlying OCI resources use the Dedicated Cloud contract discount. Oracle software license discounts cannot be applied through a paid Marketplace listing, but customers can use the BYOL version when they have an eligible existing license.

No separate country validation is required for Marketplace. Dedicated Cloud customers are reviewed before contract signature to confirm that Oracle is authorized to conduct business in the applicable region, and Marketplace is covered by the same review.
