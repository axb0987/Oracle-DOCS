# Accessing the My Apps Page

Use the My Apps page to access and organize applications.
Applications that show in the My Apps page are applications to which the administrator has granted you access. Access can be granted to you as an individual user or to a group to which you belong. You are directed to the My Apps page after you activate your account and each time you sign in thereafter.

- Open your profile console page. If you need help finding your profile console page, see[Getting Your Profile Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/usersettings/get-details-my-profile.htm).
- From Actions select View my apps console .
- Search for applications by entering a string that begins the application name.
- Set your favorites.
- Sort applications by Name and Recently granted .
