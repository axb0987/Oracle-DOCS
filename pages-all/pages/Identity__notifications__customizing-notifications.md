# Customizing Notifications

Learn how to customize the email notifications sent by an identity domain.

## Required Policy or Role

To manage identity domain settings, you must have one of the following access grants:
- Be a member of the Administrators group
- Be granted the Identity Domain Administrator role or the Security Administrator role
- Be a member of a group granted`manage`domains

To understand more about policies and roles, see[The Administrators Group, Policy, and Administrator Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/notifications/../getstarted/identity-domains.htm#The),[Understanding Administrator Roles](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/notifications/../roles/understand-administrator-roles.htm), and[IAM Policies Overview](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/notifications/../policieshow/Policy_Basics.htm).
