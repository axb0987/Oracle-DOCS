## Marketplace Publisher

Marketplace Publisher enables you to publish listings to Oracle Cloud Marketplace. To register as a marketplace publisher, you must register as an Oracle Cloud Marketplace publisher before you publish your applications and services on Oracle Cloud Marketplace.
