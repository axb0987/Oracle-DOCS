# Deleting a Terms of Use Document

Delete a terms of use document for a domain.
Remove all application references to a terms of use before you delete the terms of use. Otherwise, the only way you can delete a terms of use is to force the deletion.
Note  
  
You can't delete the default terms of use document.

- On the Terms of use documents list page, find the terms of use to delete. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Terms of Use Documents](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/termsofuse/list-terms-of-use.htm#console).
- Select the Actions menu (three dots) then select Delete terms of use document .
