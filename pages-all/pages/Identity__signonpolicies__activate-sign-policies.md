# Activating a Sign-On Policy

After you create a sign-on policy, you must activate the policy to begin enforcing it in the identity domain in IAM. You must activate a sign-on policy after you create it.

- On the Sign-on policies list page, select the sign-on policy that you want to activate. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Sign-On Policies](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/signonpolicies/list-signon-policy.htm).
- In the details page, from Actions menu, select Activate sign-on policy .
