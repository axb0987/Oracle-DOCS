# Remove a terms of use statement

The statement in a terms of use document provides the text that users in an identity domai must agree to when they first open any apps assigned to the terms of use. If you no longer want to use a particular statement, you can remove it from the terms of use document.

- On the Terms of use documents list page, select the terms of use that has the terms of use statement that you want to remove. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Terms of Use Documents](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/termsofuse/list-terms-of-use.htm#console).
- Perform one of the following actions depending on what you see:
- On the Terms of use statements tab, find the statement that you want to remove.
- Under Resources on the terms of use details page, select Terms of use statements and find the statement that you want to remove.
- Select the Actions menu (three dots) and select Remove terms of use statement .
