# Facilities, Environmental Health, and Safety

Facilities readiness covers the room, delivery route, rack environment, and life safety, electrical, and mechanical systems that support long-term operation.

## Room Construction and Delivery Path

Use the following requirements for room construction and the delivery path.

Topic Requirement Details
Room construction Build interior walls from slab to slab and meet applicable building and fire codes. Door hinges, locks, and handles must be bolted from the inside or otherwise face the protected side.
Delivery path Size doorways and hallways from the loading dock to the uncrating area for palletized rack movement. Support US-standard 1.21 m, or 48 in, pallets or 800 mm EUR pallets. Before uncrating, provide at least 2.6 m, or 8.5 ft, of overhead clearance.
Post-uncrating access Ensure that racks can move on casters from the receiving area into the final room. After uncrating, doors must have at least 2.45 m, or 8 ft, of clear height. Double doors must have no fixed center post or must have a removable center post.
Doors and egress Use controlled-entry doors for data hall and storage areas and protect emergency exits. Doors that lead to the data hall and storage area must be self-closing. Egress doors must be fire-rated and fitted with alarmed panic bars or crash bars.
Loading dock support Provide a delivery area that supports rack receiving in all weather conditions. Oracle personnel must have access to the loading dock, a covered unpacking area, and a practical path for waste removal.
Freight elevator Where an elevator is part of the delivery path, size the elevator for palletized rack movement. Minimum clear height must be 2.3 m, or 7.5 ft. Capacity must support a rack plus 115 kg of handling margin.

## Data Hall Build and Rack Area

Use the following requirements for the data hall and rack area.

Topic Requirement Details
Cage or suite standard Where Oracle requires a dedicated cage or suite, use a slab-to-slab secure boundary. Use tamper-proof fasteners, heavy-gauge steel of at least 12 gauge, and mesh openings no larger than 1.9 cm, or 0.75 in. Where slab-to-slab construction is not practical, use a caged roof or laser curtain.
Floor and ramp Design the floor and access ramps for the approved rack weight and safe movement. Perforated tiles must be non-particulate, vinyl-covered, and anti-static. Ramps must be accessible and must not exceed a 1:12 incline.
Rack envelope Plan around Oracle rack dimensions when you verify clearances and row layouts. Standard server rack: 600 mm x 1,200 mm x 2,000 mm. Standard network rack: 800 mm x 1,219 mm x 2,055 mm.
Seismic and service access Align rack positions so under-floor access remains serviceable and apply regional seismic requirements. Floor bracing must meet the site seismic zone rating, and row placement must preserve access to removable floor tiles where applicable.
Air containment Provide hot-aisle and cold-aisle containment that spans the full Oracle row. Containment must have less than 5% leakage, self-closing doors, a higher-pressure cold aisle, at least 1.21 m, or 48 in, of aisle width, and at least 90 cm behind the racks.
Cabling restrictions Connect only OCI-approved power and network cabling to OCI hardware. Facility staff, customers, and non-contracted third parties must not connect power, network, or monitoring cabling directly to OCI racks.

## Life Safety, Electrical, and Cooling Systems

Use the following requirements for systems that support Oracle equipment.

Topic Requirement Details
Lighting and signage Provide emergency lighting and label major infrastructure elements so the room can be operated safely. When occupied, target about 500 lux horizontal and 200 lux vertical lighting at 1 m above the finished floor. Label exits, egress maps, valves, cable paths, detectors, drains, emergency power off (EPO) stations, and electrical distribution.
Fire suppression and detection Use code-compliant detection and suppression designed for critical spaces. Fire suppression must comply with NFPA 75 or local code, smoke detection must comply with NFPA 72 or local code, and pre-action systems must use double-interlock protection. Extinguishers must comply with NFPA 10 or local code.
Electrical resilience Design the electrical plant without single points of failure. N+1 or 2N architectures are expected. We recommend double-conversion UPS and lightning protection to protect Oracle equipment from brownouts, frequency variation, storms, and distribution faults.
Branch circuit options Provide supported branch circuit and receptacle configurations for server and network racks. Supported examples include 380-415 V or 220-240 V wye at 30 A or 32 A with IEC309 32 A 5-pin IP44, and 200-210 V delta at 60 A or 63 A with IEC309 60 A 4-pin IP67.
Grounding Install a signal reference grid (SRG) and bond telecommunications and GPS infrastructure correctly. Grounding must follow local code and TIA-607. Provide a minimum #8 AWG copper ground in the GPS weatherproof box and bond the cable tray to the SRG.
Mechanical systems Complete the cooling and environmental support systems before Oracle equipment is deployed. Provide leak detection in IT spaces, maintain positive data-floor pressurization, and complete the final airflow design around hot-aisle and cold-aisle containment.
Commissioning Finish commissioning all systems that support Oracle equipment before deployment. Electrical, mechanical, building management system (BMS), and life-safety systems must be fully functional, meet design intent, and remain tolerant during failure conditions.
