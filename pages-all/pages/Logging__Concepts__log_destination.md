# Log Destination

You can choose the exact log group and log object where you want your log events to be indexed.

All incoming log events from your hosts are ingested and indexed in your selected log object. After they are ingested, you can view and search your log events on the Search page (see[Logging Search](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Logging/Concepts/searchinglogs.htm)
