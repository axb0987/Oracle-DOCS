# Service Request Readiness

This section defines the operational data that customers and operators must provide so Oracle can triage and resolve SRs efficiently.

SR Data Area What to Include
Problem description Provide a concise but complete description of the issue, the affected service or product area, and the observed symptoms.
Business impact State the business impact, affected production function, urgency, and why the selected severity is appropriate.
Resource identifiers Include relevant OCIDs, service instance names, tenancy or compartment context, region, timestamps, and request IDs where available.
Contacts Provide current contact information. For 24x7 work, identify separate primary and secondary contacts who can participate in troubleshooting.
Diagnostics Attach diagnostic data, such as exact error text, relevant logs, screenshots, reproduction steps, prior troubleshooting actions, and results.
Data handling Use the appropriate attachment category or internal process for files that contain personal information, confidential information, regulated data, or health information.
Escalation readiness Escalate when an SR is not progressing against project, implementation, upgrade, or business-critical milestones. Be prepared to provide business impact, SR number, and contact details.
