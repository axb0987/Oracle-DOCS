# Oracle Alloy Pricing

Oracle Alloy pricing uses price lists as the baseline catalog of stock keeping units (SKUs) and charges for Oracle Alloy services and resources. Price lists are uploaded and maintained in the Fusion Console. Price lists can be viewed in the Operator Console so that you can review available SKUs, filter by category, SKU, or product name, and identify the correct items before configuring discount logic.

Pricing rules apply discounts to subscriptions. The supported rule types are contractual discounts and promotional programs. A rule can apply to one subscription or all subscriptions. A rule can apply to all products or to selected products by SKU or discount group. When you target SKUs, enter the SKUs directly or upload a CSV file that contains the SKU list.

Pricing-rule effective dates are controlled by start and end dates. The start date must be the next day or later. When rule date ranges overlap, only one rule applies at a time. The rule with the greater discount percentage takes precedence.

When an existing rule must be changed and cannot be edited directly, clone the rule and adjust the new copy. Then disable the original rule after the replacement is in place. Disabled pricing rules do not become inactive immediately and can remain active for up to 1 hour after they are disabled.
