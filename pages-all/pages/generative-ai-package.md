## Generative AI

Generative AI is a fully managed OCI service that provides a set of state-of-the-art, customizable large language models (LLMs) that cover a wide range of use cases for text generation. Use the playground to try out the ready-to-use pretrained models, or create and host your own fine-tuned custom models based on your own data on dedicated AI clusters.
