# Commercial Systems

Oracle Alloy includes built-in commercial capabilities for orders, subscriptions, billing, rate cards, currencies, commitment-based offers, and pay-as-you-go consumption. Commitment plans can be billed monthly or yearly. End customers can move between pay-as-you-go subscriptions and commitment-based subscriptions without changing the tenancy or existing resources, unless the subscription is terminated.

Commercial design is part of the platform architecture. Packaging, billing frequency, currency strategy, discounting, quota policies, and support entitlements determine how the service is sold, governed, and expanded.
