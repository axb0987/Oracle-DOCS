# Listing Email Domains

List email domains in Email Delivery.

- [Console](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/list-email-domains.htm#)
- [CLI](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/list-email-domains.htm#)
- [API](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Reference/list-email-domains.htm#)
- 

- Open the navigation menu and select Developer Services . Under Application Integration , select Email Delivery . Under Email Delivery , select Email Domains .

The Email Delivery list page opens. All email domains in the selected compartment and region are displayed in a table.
- To view the resources in a different compartment, use the Compartment filter to switch compartments. You must have permission to work in a compartment to see the resources in it. If you're not sure which compartment to use, contact an administrator. For more information, see[Understanding Compartments](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/GSG/Concepts/settinguptenancy.htm#Understa).
- To find an email domain by name, enter the name in the search box above the table.
