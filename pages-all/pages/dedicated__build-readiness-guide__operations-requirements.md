# Operations Requirements

A site is operationally ready when it can support incidents, planned maintenance, growth, and repairs without reducing the resilience of the Oracle environment.

Operational Area Expectation Timing Or Detail
Incident response Maintain 24x7 on-call technical response for the data center environment. Provide a practical notification method so the response team can be reached quickly when assistance is needed.
Growth and improvement support Support facility and equipment upgrades required for expansion, reliability improvements, and specification changes. The site must accommodate future changes without blocking Oracle serviceability.
Critical spares Keep an appropriate range of critical facility spares onsite. The facility must demonstrate that spares are available for routine maintenance and failure recovery.
Planned maintenance Operate a formal maintenance program for critical infrastructure. Provide at least 4 weeks of notice before work that affects redundancy for infrastructure that supports Oracle racks.
Unscheduled critical maintenance Control changes that reduce resilience when the changes are not part of the annual maintenance plan. Provide at least 2 weeks of notice before unscheduled critical maintenance that reduces redundancy.
