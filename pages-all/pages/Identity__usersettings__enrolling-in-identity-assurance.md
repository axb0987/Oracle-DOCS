# Enrolling in Identity Assurance

Enroll into Identity Assurance using My profile.
Before you begin:
- Have a valid government-issued identification (ID), such as a US driver's license, a US state ID, or a passport.
- Ensure that your environment has adequate lighting.
- Have your phone available for identity verification.

You can enroll in Identity Assurance by using either of the following methods:
- During single sign-on (SSO): Follow the inline enrollment prompt when you sign in. You must enroll during the sign in process if your administrator has configured mandatory enrollment for Identity Assurance Identity Assurance during sign in.
- From My profile: Use the steps in this topic. If your domain administrator has configured Identity Assurance policy without mandatory inline enrollment, you will need to enroll from My profile to confirm your identity.

To enroll in Identity Assurance using My profile:

- In the navigation menu , select the Profile menu and then select User settings . In the My profile console, select Security .
- In the Identity Assurance section, on the Enroll with facial biometrics tile, select Enroll .
- Review the Privacy Agreement , conform your consent while selecting the button, then select Agree and continue .
- Select Start .

Tip  
  
For best results, use your phone for verification.
- Using your phone, scan the quick response (QR) code shown on your computer screen.
- If prompted, in your phone's browser, allow access to the camera and location services.
- When prompted, position your face in the frame so the system can capture your photo.
- Select the document type for your government-issued ID, then scan the front and back of the ID.

Note  
  
Ensure that the room is well lit when you scan your ID.
You can view the verification status on the next screen.
- After identity verification completes, return to the desktop browser and select Continue to start facial biometrics enrollment.
- When prompted, allow camera access, position your face in front of the camera, and select Start enrollment .
- Align your nose with each dot that appears on the screen.
