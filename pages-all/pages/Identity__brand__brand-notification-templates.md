# Adding a Logo to Email Notification Templates

Brand notification templates to display a company logo in the header and footer regions of email notifications sent to users in this identity domain.

- Open the navigation menu and select Identity &amp; Security . Under Identity , select Domains .
- Click the name of the identity domain that you want to work in. You might need to change the compartment to find the domain that you want.
- Select Branding .
- If Oracle branding (default) is selected, then select Custom branding .
- Under Email templates , upload a header logo to display in the header region of all email notifications from this identity domain.
- When you're finished, select Save changes .
