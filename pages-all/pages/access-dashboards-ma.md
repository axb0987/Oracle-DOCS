# Access Dashboards

You must access the Dashboards page to view Oracle-defined dashboards for Management Agent and create custom dashboards.

To access dashboards in Management Agent:
- Sign in to the Oracle Cloud Infrastructure console.
- Open the navigation menu, click Observability &amp; Management and then select Management Dashboards .
On the Dashboards page, the list of Oracle-defined dashboards and custom dashboards, if any, is displayed.
- To view dashboards for Management Agent, use the Search and Filter bar and select the service as`AGENT`.

You can use the Oracle-defined dashboards for Management Agent to monitor the health and performance of your management agents or create custom dashboards that meet your specific requirements.

On the left pane, under Dashboards :
- Click Widgets to view the list of available widgets in the widget library for a supported Observability &amp; Management service. For information, see[View Widgets](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/doc/view-widgets.html#GUID-C7FEAAAB-6DBB-479A-B120-D2AAB54C9D96)in the Management Dashboard documentation.
- Click Filters to view the list of available filters in the filter library for a supported Observability &amp; Management service. For information, see[View Filters](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/doc/view-filters.html#GUID-84A3B2AD-2C75-447D-9200-E893CD825FF8)in the Management Dashboard documentation.
