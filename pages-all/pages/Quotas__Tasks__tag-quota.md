# Tagging a Quota

Describes how to add tagging metadata to a quota. You can define keys and values, and associate them with resources.

You can apply tags to a quota's resources to help you organize them according to your business needs. Apply tags at the time you create a quota, or update the quota with tags later. For more information about applying tags, see[Overview of Tagging](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Tagging/Concepts/taggingoverview.htm).

The following sections describe how you can apply tags to quotas:
- [Tagging a Quota at Creation](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Quotas/Tasks/tag-quota-create.htm)
- [Tagging a Quota When Updating](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Quotas/Tasks/tag-quota-update.htm)
