On the parent tenancy Subscription Mapping list page, select the subscription that you want to work with. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Subscriptions](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/General/organization/list-subscriptions.htm).

The subscription mapping details page displays the subscription details.

The Mapped tenancies tab or section displays the tenancies mapped to the subscription and the date they were mapped.
