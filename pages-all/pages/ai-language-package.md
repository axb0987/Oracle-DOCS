## AI Language

AI Language is a serverless and multi-tenant service that is accessible using REST API calls. It provides pre-trained models that are frequently retrained and monitored to give you the best results. Language provides you with artificial intelligence and machine learning capabilities to detect the language in your unstructured text. Also, it provides other tools to help you further gain insights into your text.
