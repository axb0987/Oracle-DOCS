To review requirements for working with Kafka Connect, see[Using Kafka Connect](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Streaming/Tasks/kafkacompatibility_topic-Kafka_Connect.htm).

- [Getting a Kafka Connect Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Streaming/Tasks/get-connect-harness.htm)
- [Updating a Kafka Connect Configuration](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Streaming/Tasks/update-connect-harness.htm)
