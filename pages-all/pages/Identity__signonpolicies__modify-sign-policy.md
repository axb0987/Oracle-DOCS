# Updating a Sign-On Policy

Update a sign-on policy in an identity domain in IAM.

- On the Sign-on policies list page, select the sign-on policy that you want to update. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Sign-On Policies](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/signonpolicies/list-signon-policy.htm).
- On the details page, select Actions and then select Edit Sign-on rule .
- Update the settings as needed. Avoid entering confidential information. For descriptions of the settings, see[Creating a Sign-On Policy](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/signonpolicies/add-sign-policy.htm).
