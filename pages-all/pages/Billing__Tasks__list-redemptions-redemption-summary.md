Use the Rewards Redemption History page to view the redemption activity against the accrued Oracle Support Rewards. You can view the history by subscription and time period.

- Open the navigation menu and select Billing &amp; Cost Management . Under Programs and Rewards , select Rewards Redemption History .
- On the Rewards Redemption History page, for Subscription , select a subscription to view its history. The field also shows the subscription ID in parentheses.
- Select a time period from the corresponding Time period field.
- To download a CSV copy of the rewards redemption history, select Export to CSV .
