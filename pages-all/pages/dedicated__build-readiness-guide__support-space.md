# Support Space

Dedicated Cloud deployments need more than rack positions. The site must provide secure support areas for spares, staging, receiving, and daily operational handling.

Area Requirement Details
Dedicated storage A secure storage room can be used for spare equipment and parts in non-contiguous deployments. Provide at least 9.25 sq m, or 100 sq ft, for Oracle use only. The room must be secure, slab-to-slab, and located as close to the data hall as practical.
Integrated spares racks Integrated spares racks can be used instead of, or in addition to, a dedicated storage room. Deployments of up to 50 server or network racks require two more rack positions for spares. For every 25 more racks, plan one more spare rack position with network and power.
Staging and build area A dedicated secure staging area is preferred for the initial build and larger expansion events. Flooring must have anti-static properties to ASTM E649 Class 1, and the temperature must remain within ASHRAE guidance.
Shipping and receiving The loading dock and receiving area must support large deliveries during and outside business hours. Provide 24x7x365 loading dock access. In Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), the site must also receive EUR pallets.
Rack handling Verify that the receiving path can accept fully integrated Oracle racks. The site must receive racks up to 900 kg or 1,150 kg, depending on the deployment footprint.
