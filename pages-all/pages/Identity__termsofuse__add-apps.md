# Adding Apps to a Terms of Use Document

Assign an application to a terms of use document for an identity domain in IAM.

- On the Terms of use documents list page, select the terms of use document to add an app to. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Terms of Use Documents](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/termsofuse/list-terms-of-use.htm#console).
- Perform one of the following actions depending on what you see:
- On the Apps tab, select Add app .
- Under Resources , select Apps , and then select Add app .
