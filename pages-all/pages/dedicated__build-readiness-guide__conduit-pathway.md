# Conduit Pathway

Conduit and pathway design must protect cabling, preserve diversity, and keep routes serviceable over time.

Requirement Area Requirement Details
Primary pathway design Use diverse, tamperproof secure metal pathways between Oracle space and the MMR or equivalent room. The primary preference is two 107 mm, or 4 in, metal conduits, each with Plenum MaxCell Fabric Innerduct. Armored fiber is the secondary preference where conduit is not available.
Pathway spacing Keep diverse routes physically separated through the building and between buildings. Maintain at least 10 m of separation for inter-building paths and at least 2 m for intra-building paths.
Junction and bend control Design the route so cable installation and future service work remain practical. Use junction boxes at least every 30 m and after two 90-degree bends. Use long-radius sweeps. For 4 in conduit, the bend radius must be at least 914.4 mm, or 36 in.
Fill and penetration control Avoid overfilling conduit and protect all wall penetrations. Fill ratio must stay below 40%, and wall penetrations must be fire-stopped to local code.
Electromagnetic interference separation Apply electrical separation rules where communications and power paths run near each other. Follow NFPA 70. Zero separation is permitted only where metallic pathways are properly bonded and grounded. Parallel twisted-pair and shielded power cables or busway must maintain 300 mm, or 12 in, of separation.
GPS pathway Provide the rooftop routing and grounding support required for GPS timing equipment. Install at least 41 mm, or 1.5 in, conduit to the rooftop GPS mounting area. Terminate the conduit in a 609 mm x 609 mm x 152 mm weatherproof metal box and provide a building ground on a bus bar in that box.
Overhead cable tray Use an overhead basket tray system where rack-above cabling is planned. Install basket tray at a minimum height of 2.44 m, or 8 ft. Provide waterfall spill-outs at drop locations and plan around typical tray sizes of 152 mm x 457 mm for main pathways and 152 mm x 609 mm for row pathways.
