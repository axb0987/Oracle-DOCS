# Listing Quotas

List all quota policies on the Quota policies page.

- [Console](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Quotas/Tasks/list-quota.htm#)
- [CLI](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Quotas/Tasks/list-quota.htm#)
- [API](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Quotas/Tasks/list-quota.htm#)
- 

Open the navigation menu and select Governance &amp; Administration . Under Tenancy Management , select Quota policies .

(Optional) Search for and select the compartment from the list of Applied filters . The page updates to show only the quotas in that compartment.

All the quota policies are displayed by quota name, status, and creation date.
