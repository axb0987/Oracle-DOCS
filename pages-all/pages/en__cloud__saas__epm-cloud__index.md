# Oracle Cloud Enterprise Performance Management (EPM)

## Get Started with Business Processes

### Connected Planning
Oracle Cloud EPM helps align planning across the enterprise. Respond to change faster—and more effectively—by developing agile forecasts for all lines of business. Create long-range forecasts for unpredictable business dynamics using sophisticated, built-in scenario modeling. Gain insight into dimensions of cost and profitability to determine where to invest limited resources. FreeForm business process due to its flexible dimension structure is amenable to any business planning and reporting process that needs to be connected.
- [Planning](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/planning-budgeting-cloud/index.html)
- [FreeForm](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/freeform/index.html)
- Profitability and Cost Management is a business process with two application types:
- [Enterprise Profitability and Cost Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-profitability-cost-management-cloud/index.html)
- [Profitability and Cost Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/profit-cost-cloud/index.html)

### Connected Financial Close
Oracle Cloud EPM enables organizations to adapt quickly to changing business and compliance requirements while reducing risk, improving controls, and delivering faster, more accurate insights to all stakeholders—anytime, anywhere.
- [Account Reconciliation](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/account-reconcile-cloud/index.html)
- [Financial Consolidation and Close](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/financial-consolidation-cloud/index.html)
- [Tax Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/tax-reporting-cloud/index.html)

### Reporting
With Narrative Reporting, businesses of all sizes can streamline reporting processes and combine data plus narrative in a secure, collaborative environment to deliver faster and more accurate insights for all stakeholders—even on the go. FreeForm business process allows businesses to create a unified reporting solution across multiple internal and external sources using the robust capabilities within Narrative Reporting and leverage visualization, analysis and reporting capabilities within Cloud EPM Platform.
- [Narrative Reporting](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-performance-reporting-cloud/index.html)
- [FreeForm](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/freeform/index.html)

### Cloud Enterprise Data Management
Cloud Enterprise Data Management provides a single platform to flexibly manage enterprise data while maintaining data integrity and enterprise application alignment. With improved data management, you can drive agile business transformation. Cloud Enterprise Data Management is a Cloud service, and has no restrictions on hosted employee and record metrics.  
  
The Enterprise Data Management business process is part of the EPM Enterprise subscription, and provides enough records for a capability preview only.
- [Cloud Enterprise Data Management](https://docs.oracle.com/en/cloud/saas/enterprise-data-management-cloud/index.html)
