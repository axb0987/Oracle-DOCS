# Deactivating a Terms of Use Document

Deactivate a terms of use document for an identity domain in IAM.

- On the Terms of use documents list page, find the terms of use document to deactivate. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Terms of Use Documents](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/termsofuse/list-terms-of-use.htm#console).
- Select the Actions menu (three dots) and select Deactivate terms of use document .
