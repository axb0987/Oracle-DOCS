# Subscriptions and Payment Failure

When failing to pay your Oracle Universal Credits bill, access to OCI services and resources can be restricted based on your subscriptions.

When a customer fails to pay their bill but only has an[Oracle Universal Credits](https://www.oracle.com/cloud/universal-credits/)subscription, then the tenancy and cloud account, and all associated resources, are suspended.

If a customer has both Oracle Universal Credits and non-Oracle Universal Credits subscriptions, however, then the following conditions apply until the bill is paid:
- The tenancy and cloud account remain active.
- The tenancy has reduced limits and can't start any new resources, and can only use resources not covered by Oracle Universal Credits.
