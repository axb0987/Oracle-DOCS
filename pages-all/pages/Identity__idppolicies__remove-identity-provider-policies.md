# Deleting an Identity Provider Policy

Delete an identity provider (IdP) policy from an identity domain in IAM.

- On the Identity provider (IdP) policies list page, find and open the policy to delete. If you need help finding the list page, see[Listing Identity Provider Policies](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/idppolicies/list-idp-policies.htm#console).
- If the policy has apps assigned to it, remove the apps from the policy.
- Under Resources , select Apps .
- Select the checkbox for each app that you want to remove from the policy.
- Select Remove app .
- To confirm the removal, select Remove app .
- Select Remove IdP policy .
