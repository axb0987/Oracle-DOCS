# Using SMTP for Email Submissions

Use Simple Mail Transfer Protocol (SMTP) submission to send secure bulk marketing and transactional emails and ensure that you have the best-sending reputation.

To submit bulk emails, SMTP submissions require an SMTP credential. Ensure that the submission connections are encrypted before credentials are sent, and they're only supported over secure,[Authentication and Connection Endpoints](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Email/Concepts/overview.htm#SMTP_Authentication_and_Connection_Endpoints).
