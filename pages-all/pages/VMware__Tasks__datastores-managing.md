# Managing VMware Solution Datastores

VMware Solution includes several ways to manage dastastores.

You can perform the following datastore management tasks:

[List the datastores in a compartment](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-list.htm).

[Create a datastore](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-create.htm).

[Get a datastore's details](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-get.htm).

[Rename a datastore](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-update.htm).

[Move a datastore to a different compartment](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-change-compartment.htm).

[Delete a datastore from your tenancy](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/VMware/Tasks/datastore-delete.htm)
