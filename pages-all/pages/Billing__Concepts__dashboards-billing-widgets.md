# Console Dashboards Integration

You can use Console Dashboards to get a quick view of billing and cost management information in your tenancy.

Use the following billing and cost management widgets on your dashboard:
- The[Infrastructure Billing Widget](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Dashboards/Tasks/widgetmanagement.htm#billing)widget lets you quickly view your current charges or usage and the days elapsed in your billing cycle, depending on the account type.
- The[Cost Management Widget](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Dashboards/Tasks/widgetmanagement.htm#cost-management)widget integrates with[Cost Analysis](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Billing/Concepts/costanalysisoverview.htm)and helps you track and optimize your Oracle Cloud Infrastructure spending, by generating charts and reports of aggregated cost data.

See[Overview of the Console Dashboards Service](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Dashboards/Concepts/dashboardsoverview.htm)and[Configuring Widgets](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Dashboards/Tasks/widgetmanagement.htm)
