# Unlinking a My Oracle Cloud Support Account

Unlink your identity domains profile from a My Oracle Cloud Support account .

- Open your profile console page. If you need help finding your profile console page, see[Getting Your Profile Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/usersettings/get-details-my-profile.htm).
- From Actions select Unlink support account .
