# Editing Your Primary Email

You can change the primary email address that was set up for you when your account was created.

The primary email address is the email address to which all your notifications are sent. Your administrator has already set your primary email address.

- Open your profile console page. If you need help finding your profile console page, see[Getting Your Profile Details](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Identity/usersettings/get-details-my-profile.htm).
- From the Actions menu select Edit email .
- On the Email window, select the the Actions menu (three dots) , select Change . Enter your current password and select Verify .
- Enter the new primary email.
- Select Change primary email .

A verification email is sent to your new email address. To verify your email address, follow the instructions in the email. Also, an update email notification is sent to your old email address.
