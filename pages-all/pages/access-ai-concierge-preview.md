# Access AI Concierge (Preview)

AI Concierge provides administrators and consumers AI-powered capabilities in Oracle Fusion Data Intelligence. You can ask questions in conversational English, and receive real-time information related to configurations specific to the instance and get assistance for your queries.

The AI Concierge experience for administrators and consumers depends on their privileges. Consumers can only make informational queries. They aren't allowed to take any action such as enabling features.
Note  
  
Security Disclaimer: AI Concierge requests are always processed within a secure Oracle Cloud Infrastructure environment. Your AI Concierge request may be routed to another Oracle Cloud Infrastructure region for processing.
