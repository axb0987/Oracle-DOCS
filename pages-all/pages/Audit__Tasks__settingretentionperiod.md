# Audit Log Retention Period

Describes the Audit log retention period.

Audit logs are retained for 365 days. You can view the log retention period in the tenancy details page. For more information, see[Viewing Tenancy Details](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/General/tenancy/managingtenancy.htm).
