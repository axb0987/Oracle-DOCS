# Roles and Responsibilities

The Oracle Alloy operator owns the direct relationship with end customers, including contracts, onboarding, pricing, billing, support engagement, and service presentation. Oracle contracts with the Oracle Alloy operator rather than with the end customer. End customers can still maintain separate Oracle licensing relationships for supported bring-your-own-license scenarios.

Oracle provides the cloud platform, operational tooling, training, and Tier 2 and Tier 3 support to the operator. The operator provides Tier 1 support, manages end-customer communications, and remains accountable for the end-to-end customer experience.

When the operator also serves as the internet transit provider, the operator is responsible for peering, route advertisement, firewall and ACL implementation, and customer-side redundancy that prevents a single point of failure.

The platform does not support multitier partner-to-partner commercial routes to market. Referral arrangements are handled outside the platform and do not change the requirement that the Oracle Alloy operator retains commercial control and Tier 1 support responsibility.
