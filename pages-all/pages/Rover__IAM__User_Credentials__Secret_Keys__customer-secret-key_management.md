# Customer Secret Keys for Roving Edge Infrastructure Devices

Learn about customer secret key management tasks for users on your Roving Edge Infrastructure device.

You can perform the following customer secret keys management tasks:
- 

[Creating Customer Secret Keys](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Rover/IAM/User_Credentials/Secret_Keys/create_customer-secret-key.htm#CreateCustomerSecretKey)
- 

[Listing Customer Secret Keys](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Rover/IAM/User_Credentials/Secret_Keys/list_customer-secret-key.htm#ListCustomerSecretKey)
- 

[Editing Customer Secret Keys](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Rover/IAM/User_Credentials/Secret_Keys/update_customer-secret-key.htm#UpdateCustomerSecretKey)
- 

[Deleting Customer Secret Keys](https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/Rover/IAM/User_Credentials/Secret_Keys/delete_customer-secret-key.htm#DeleteCustomerSecretKey)

A customer secret key consists of an access key/secret key pair. Oracle automatically generates the access key when you or your administrator generates the secret key to create the customer secret key.

See[Working with Customer Secret Keys](https://docs.oracle.com/iaas/Content/Identity/Tasks/managingcredentials.htm#Working2)
