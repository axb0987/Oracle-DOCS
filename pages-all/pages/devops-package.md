## DevOps

DevOps is a continuous integration/continuous delivery (CI/CD) service that automates the delivery and deployment of software to Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (OCI) compute platforms.

DevOps enables you to develop, build, and deploy workloads at scale quickly. The DevOps deployment pipeline reduces change-driven errors and decreases the time customers spend on deploying releases.
